The Philosophy

Born of Embers, Sustained by Grace.

Founded by Chef Julian Vance, Aura & Ember strips away the sterile formality of modern dining. Instead, we return to the primal magic of fire, seasoned timber, and locally harvested ingredients.

Each evening, a 9-course tasting journey is presented alongside curated pairings from independent biodynamic winemakers and rare cellar selections.
